Kada Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Kada records voice notes on iPhone and Apple Watch. Recording works without an account. Cloud transcription and connected agents are optional account features. This policy explains what stays on your devices and what is sent to the Kada service.

Information stored on your devices

Your recordings, playback files, and recording context are saved on the device that recorded them. The iPhone and Watch keep independent recordings; audio is not transferred directly between them. Context includes time, time zone, device and battery details, recording events, and audio format. If you separately enable the relevant sources, it can also include location, movement and activity, and HealthKit readings and saved workouts. Kada reads HealthKit data but does not write to HealthKit. Location, motion, and health collection are off until you enable them.

Information sent to Cloud

If you sign in on iPhone or pair a Watch with your account, new recordings can upload automatically for transcription after you accept the Cloud disclosure. You can decline and keep recordings on the device; the next new recording asks again. Starting with iPhone version 1.1.2, new iPhone Cloud recordings include their complete saved recording context, including any optional sensor data you chose to collect, uploaded after the audio. Upgrading requires renewed Cloud consent before uploads resume. There is no iPhone context-sharing switch. Apple Watch context sharing remains an independent choice, off by default. Recordings made before you signed in or paired upload only when you choose them. Existing recordings are not retroactively opted into context sharing, and selecting audio from an older recording does not automatically share its context.

The service stores uploaded audio, note titles and other content, transcripts, summaries, edits, and the context you choose to share. It also receives your search terms when you search Cloud notes. Your account uses an email address and an account identifier; sign-in and agent authorization require authentication data. The app stores its session credential in the device Keychain. The service and its hosting providers may keep technical request logs, which can include Cloud search requests.

How information is used and shared

We use this information to store and play your notes, transcribe and organize them, provide account and agent access, operate the service, and protect it from abuse. For mainland China accounts, Alibaba Cloud Model Studio in Beijing processes audio and note text using Qwen services for transcription, clean-up, titles, summaries, translation, and related-note organization, including embeddings. Global accounts use OpenAI for these AI services. The join judge is self-hosted. Hosting and storage providers also process information to operate Cloud. These AI processing locations do not describe the location of all Kada hosting or storage. We do not sell your recordings or use them for advertising or cross-app tracking.

The account's AI region is assigned when the account is created. On iPhone, account-creation requests send the App Store country reported by StoreKit, when available; the server uses this to assign the region. When that value is unavailable, the server uses its country fallback. This is the App Store account country, not GPS location or the phone's language. Existing accounts retain their assigned AI region. Paired Watch uploads follow their account's region. The iPhone's Cloud upload disclosure and Help show the provider returned by the account's settings.

When you approve a connected agent, it can access only the note, transcript, metadata, or write permissions you selected. In Kada 1.1.1, you can revoke an agent’s access in the Agents tab. Earlier versions offer Settings → Manage connected agents.

Keeping and deleting information

On iPhone and Apple Watch, uploaded local audio is automatically deleted 30 days after Kada confirms receipt of the complete recording. Cleanup runs when the app next becomes active, including offline or signed out, and does not require Cloud audio to remain available. Unuploaded, unfinished, active, and playing recordings are protected. Automatic audio cleanup retains local recording context and upload receipts. You can also swipe left in Local recordings on iPhone to delete a recording and its local context; this does not delete a Cloud copy. Turning off Watch context sharing affects future uploads and does not remove context already shared. Delete the Cloud note to remove shared context.

Kada deletes Cloud audio after transcription completes. If transcription fails, the audio is kept so you can retry. Transcripts, summaries, and Cloud notes remain in your account until you delete them. Deleting a Cloud note hides it immediately and schedules any remaining stored audio for removal. In the iPhone app, Settings → Account → Delete account requests deletion of the account, its Cloud notes, and stored Cloud audio; it does not delete local recordings. Copies in service provider backups and operational logs may remain after deletion according to the providers’ retention schedules. Contact us to ask about those schedules or request help deleting your information.

Your choices

  • Decline the Cloud disclosure to keep new recordings on the device. Signing out on iPhone or disconnecting the Watch also stops new automatic uploads.
  • Control optional location, motion, and health collection in Recording context or change sensor permissions in device Settings. New iPhone Cloud recordings include saved context after Cloud consent; decline Cloud uploads to keep new recordings local. Watch context sharing can be turned off separately.
  • Delete individual Cloud notes, revoke a connected agent, sign out, or delete your account in the app.
