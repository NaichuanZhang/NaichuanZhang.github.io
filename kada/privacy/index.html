Kada Privacy Policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

Kada records voice notes on iPhone and Apple Watch. Recording works without an account. Cloud transcription and connected agents are optional account features. This policy explains what stays on your devices and what is sent to the Kada service.

Using Kada with ChatGPT

Information stored on your devices

Your recordings, playback files, and recording context are saved on the device that recorded them. The iPhone and Watch keep independent recordings; audio is not transferred directly between them. Context includes time, time zone, device and battery details, recording events, and audio format. If you separately enable the relevant sources, it can also include location, movement and activity, and HealthKit readings and saved workouts. Kada reads HealthKit data but does not write to HealthKit. Location, motion, and health collection are off until you enable them.

Information sent to Cloud

Starting with iPhone version 1.2, signing in enables automatic uploads of new recordings for transcription without a separate Cloud confirmation. Record without signing in to keep new recordings on your iPhone. New iPhone Cloud recordings include their complete saved recording context, including any optional sensor data you chose to collect, uploaded after the audio. There is no iPhone context-sharing switch. On Apple Watch, pairing and accepting the Cloud disclosure enable automatic uploads; you can decline to keep recordings on the Watch, and the next new recording asks again. Watch context sharing remains an independent choice, off by default. Recordings made before you signed in or paired upload only when you choose them. Existing recordings are not retroactively opted into context sharing, and selecting audio from an older recording does not automatically share its context.

The service stores uploaded audio, note titles and other content, transcripts, summaries, edits, and the context you choose to share. It also receives your search terms when you search Cloud notes. Your account uses an email address and an account identifier. Sign-in and agent authorization require authentication data. The app stores its session credential in the device Keychain. To operate and secure the service, Kada and its hosting providers may log request times, network addresses, URLs, and response status. Cloud note search terms can appear in request URLs when you search in the web or mobile app.

How information is used and shared

We use this information to store and play your notes, transcribe and organize them, provide account and agent access, operate the service, and protect it from abuse. Oracle Cloud hosts Kada’s servers, database, audio storage, and database backups. Requests to Kada and its connector pass through Cloudflare for network delivery and security. We do not sell your recordings or use them for advertising or cross-app tracking.

For global accounts, Kada sends audio to StepFun for Cloud speech transcription. For mainland China accounts, Alibaba Cloud Model Studio in Beijing processes audio and note text for transcription and features such as clean-up, titles, summaries, and translation. If you turn on automatic note merging, Kada may send titles, summary points, and transcript excerpts from related notes to Alibaba Cloud Model Studio in Beijing to assess whether the notes belong together. This applies to both global and mainland China accounts. Connecting ChatGPT is a separate choice.

The account’s AI region is assigned when the account is created. On iPhone, account-creation requests send the App Store country reported by StoreKit when available. Otherwise, Kada uses the country supplied by its network provider where available. Existing accounts retain their assigned AI region. Paired Watch uploads follow their account’s region.

If you send feedback, Kada stores your message, category, contact email if supplied or linked to your account, and available app, browser, or device details to respond and investigate. Where issue tracking is enabled, this information is also sent to Linear. Account deletion clears the account link and contact email on Kada’s feedback record. The message itself can remain. Contact us if you want a feedback message or its support copy removed.

Using Kada with ChatGPT

When you connect Kada to ChatGPT, you sign in with Kada and choose which permissions to grant. Kada gives ChatGPT credentials for this connection. OpenAI receives the results of the tools ChatGPT calls so it can answer your questions in ChatGPT. The tools work with Cloud notes in the connected Kada account. They return text and recording details, never audio or files that exist only on your device.

ChatGPT sends Kada the inputs needed for the selected tool. Search words and proposed titles can include words from your conversation.

  • Identify the connection. whoami uses the connection credential, with no search or note input. It returns your Kada account email, the connection name, its permissions and credential expiry, and Kada’s web address so you can identify the account in use.
  • Find notes. search_notes takes search words and an optional language filter. list_recent_notes can take a time range. Both accept paging options and return IDs, titles, reader links, dates, durations, languages, status, summaries, and, with transcript access, excerpts. The recent-notes feed can also return a deleted note’s ID and deletion time so the assistant knows it is no longer available. For ChatGPT research, search takes search words and returns matching IDs, titles, and links.
  • Read or quote a note. get_note, get_transcript, and fetch take a note ID. Depending on the tool, ChatGPT can request a transcript version, original or cleaned text, timestamps, or a starting position. With the required permissions, results can include the note title and link, recording dates and time zone, language and duration, summary, transcript text including the full transcript, saved translations, and IDs of notes grouped together. Anything mentioned in the note, including details about other people, can appear in the returned text.
  • Share optional recording context. If context was uploaded with a note and you separately approve access to recording details, get_note can return that note’s saved context to OpenAI. This can include device and battery details and recording events, plus precise location, movement, HealthKit measurements such as heart rate, and saved workouts if you enabled those sources. The recording-details permission starts off and can be left off while using the transcript tools.
  • Rename a note. If you separately approve permission to rename notes, rename_note takes the note ID and the replacement title, saves that title to your Kada account, and returns the ID, title, link, and recording date to OpenAI. The title stays on the note until you change it or delete the note. Rename permission starts off.

What Kada keeps. The connector uses search words and read requests to look up your existing notes. It does not create a separate chat transcript or save a history of tool inputs and results in your library. Connector performance metrics count tool names, outcomes, and request duration without recording search terms, note contents, account IDs, or credentials. The service’s separate operational logs and database backups are described below.

To authorize access and prevent misuse, Kada stores the connected app’s identity, your account link, the approved permissions, credential hashes, and connection, use, expiry, or revocation times. Access credentials last 30 minutes and can be renewed while the connection remains active. After 90 days without renewal, the connection expires. Unusable credentials are cleaned up periodically. Revoked connection records can remain until the app’s registration is cleaned up or you delete your Kada account.

Disconnect or delete shared information. In Kada on the web or iPhone, open Agents → Connected apps and disconnect ChatGPT to stop future access. To grant fewer permissions, disconnect and reconnect with the permissions you want. Information already sent to ChatGPT can remain in past conversations after you disconnect or delete the original Kada note. You can delete those conversations in ChatGPT. OpenAI handles its copy under the terms and data controls applicable to your ChatGPT account or workspace. Manage access in Kada · OpenAI Privacy Policy · Delete a ChatGPT conversation

When you authorize a different assistant, that assistant receives the results it requests within the permissions you approved. You can revoke connected apps and other agent sign-ins in Kada’s Agents tab.

Keeping and deleting information

On iPhone and Apple Watch, uploaded local audio from successfully processed recordings normally becomes eligible for automatic deletion 30 days after Kada confirms receipt of the complete recording. Cleanup runs when the app next becomes active, including offline or signed out, and does not require Cloud audio to remain available. Unuploaded, unfinished, active, and playing recordings, and recordings with failed or unconfirmed processing, are protected. Automatic audio cleanup retains local recording context and upload receipts. You can also swipe left in Local recordings on iPhone to delete a recording and its local context; this does not delete a Cloud copy. Turning off Watch context sharing affects future uploads and does not remove context already shared. Delete the Cloud note to remove shared context.

Starting with version 1.2.2, captures with no recorded audio are discarded and their empty Cloud placeholders are canceled. A short recording with no recognized words after every segment has been successfully transcribed may be scheduled for deletion after a 24-hour grace period. The current short-recording threshold is 10 seconds. The app shows the deletion date and offers Keep recording to cancel that deadline or Delete now to request earlier deletion. A short duration alone does not cause deletion, and failed or incomplete transcription is not treated as silence. At the deadline, the Cloud note is hidden and its remaining data and audio are scheduled for removal unless you have kept it. For this short-recording cleanup, local audio is removed only after the device receives confirmation of the Cloud deletion and playback is protected; an offline device may retain a copy longer. Keep recording cancels the short-recording deadline; the normal device-audio retention policy still applies.

For recordings with transcript text, Kada deletes Cloud audio after transcription completes successfully. Audio from failed or incomplete transcriptions is kept so you can retry. Except for the short-recording cleanup described above, titles, transcripts, summaries, and uploaded context remain in your account until you delete the note or your account. Deleting a Cloud note immediately hides its contents from readers and connected apps. Its ID and deletion time remain visible to the recent-notes feed until the deletion job runs. Kada schedules removal of the note’s live database records and remaining Cloud audio after 24 hours. In the iPhone app, Settings → Account → Delete account removes the account, its connections, and its Cloud notes and audio. Recordings kept on your devices can be deleted separately.

Kada makes database backups for recovery. Its standard schedule keeps backups for 14 days and always preserves the three most recent successful backups. During a backup interruption those three may be older than 14 days. Operational logs can outlast a note or account. The standard configuration rotates application and database logs after five 10 MB files per service, so their age depends on traffic. Host system logs are normally kept for up to one month, subject to a size limit. Contact us for help with deletion or information about a particular data category.

Your choices

  • Record without signing in, or sign out on iPhone, to keep new recordings local. On Apple Watch, decline the Cloud disclosure or disconnect the Watch to stop new automatic uploads.
  • Control optional location, motion, and health collection in Recording context or change sensor permissions in device Settings. New iPhone Cloud recordings include saved context when signed in. Watch context sharing can be turned off separately.
  • Delete individual Cloud notes, revoke a connected agent, sign out, or delete your account in the app.
