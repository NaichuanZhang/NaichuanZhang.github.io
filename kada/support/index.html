Kada Support

Kada records voice notes on iPhone and Apple Watch. You can record and play local notes without an account. For help, contact alexzhang718@gmail.com.

Find your recordings

On iPhone, open Settings → Local recordings to play audio saved on that iPhone. The Library tab shows Cloud notes when you are signed in. On Apple Watch, open Kada’s recording list to play notes saved on that watch. The devices keep separate local libraries; Watch audio does not sync directly to iPhone.

Cloud and transcription

Sign in under iPhone Settings → Account. New recordings upload for transcription when Cloud upload is enabled. You can turn off automatic uploads in Settings. Earlier recordings need to be selected individually for upload. To connect an Apple Watch to your account for new uploads, open Kada on both paired devices; a pairing code is available under iPhone Settings → Apple Watch pairing if automatic pairing is unavailable. A network connection is needed for Cloud features.

Microphone and context

Allow microphone access to record. If recording was interrupted, use Settings → Check for interrupted recordings on iPhone. Location, motion, and health collection are optional and are off until enabled under Recording context. Cloud context sharing is a separate setting. If a sensor has no reading, it will not block audio recording.

Privacy and account help

See the Kada Privacy Policy for details about Cloud uploads, context sharing, and deletion. To delete your account, open iPhone Settings → Account → Delete account. Local recordings on your devices are not deleted with your Cloud account. Email alexzhang718@gmail.com for account or privacy support.
